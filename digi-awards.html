WHAT IS D.I.G.I.?

D.I.G.I. is the practice of human-led creation using digital and generative tools with intention, restraint, and direction. It recognizes inspiration as real, tools as powerful, and guidance as the deciding factor between noise and craft. Whether AI is used minimally or extensively, D.I.G.I. values transparency, taste, and authorship through steering — not outsourcing. Some call it digitally inventive creation. Others call it divinely inspired guidance. We ask one thing: was the inspiration guided into something worth finishing?

RULES (SIMPLE & REAL)

  • Original work only. You own it or have permission to submit it.
  • Link-based submissions (YouTube, Suno, SoundCloud, etc.).
  • AI-assisted and non-AI work are both welcome. Document your process.
  • No hate content. No spam dumping. Keep it human.

D.I.G.I. doesn’t ask if AI was involved. It asks whether inspiration was guided into something finished, intentional, and worth sharing.
