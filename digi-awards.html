The Digi Awards recognize intentional digital creation, with attention to the decisions, iterations and craft that shape a finished work.

D.I.G.I.

Directed · Inventive · Guided · Inspiration

The idea

Show the work.
Share the process.

Digital and generative tools can be part of the process in many ways. D.I.G.I. asks creators to describe their contribution, the tools they used and the choices they made.

Planned categories

  • Best Song
  • Best Music Video
  • Best Genre Fusion
  • Best Satire Track
  • Most Creative Prompting
  • Least AI Assistance (Documentable)
  • Most AI Assistance (Documentable)
  • Most Efficient Prompting
  • Experimental / Boundary Pusher
  • Community Choice

Entry preparation

Keep the process visible.

  • Submit original work you own or have permission to enter.
  • Use a public work link and credit collaborators.
  • Describe the digital or generative tools used and your creative decisions.
  • Include prompts and iteration notes where useful.
  • Keep entries free of hate content and spam.

Categories and entry guidance are being developed. An opening date, judging schedule and submission destination have yet to be announced.

Prepare for entry

Keep a draft ready.

This form prepares a text file on your device. Downloading a draft does not submit it to ETS. Entries are not being accepted through this site yet.
