Brian P. Hughes & ETS

Technology
That Liberates.

ETS is a digital production studio exploring music, animation, satire and interactive experiences through human-led collaboration with AI tools.

Brian brings the ideas, direction, selection and ongoing revisions. The work develops through conversation, experiments and the pursuit of a finished experience worth sharing.

How the studio works

Direction in every detail.

Music

Write the lyric, assign the instruments their jobs, compare performances and refine the arrangement. TRF’s sound grows through the choices made between versions.

Visual storytelling

Build characters and environments, establish poses, then storyboard the action and transitions against the song or script.

Transparent creation

Keep track of the tools, iterations and creative decisions. The developing D.I.G.I. approach makes that process part of the work’s presentation.
