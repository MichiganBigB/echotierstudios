# UNfiltered — Mara / Diana / Caspian dialogue archive
Date captured: 2026-11-22
Status: Writers' room archive. Dialogue preserved from conversation; scenes are proposals, not finalized episode scripts.

## Established canon / guardrails
- Mara was alive and physically present before her final field assignment.
- She died in the field in a stupid accident during what was supposed to be her last remote before promotion to an anchor desk.
- The exact cause of the accident is **undecided**. It should look VERY suspicious, allowing the audience to suspect foul play; the underlying accident remains mundane.
- Mara has been digitized and continues remote/green-screen reporting, unaware she is dead.
- Mara remembers the promised promotion and is perpetually one story away from the desk.
- Management's rationale for not informing her: **"No reason to upset her."**
- The concealment and digital continuation are the real cover-up, distinct from the accident.
- Diana carries her microphone; Caspian is polished and can miss the implications of his own statements.
- The Feed → The Current → The Spiral is a proposed thematic framework, not necessarily a finalized literal mechanism.

## 1. Character-model mishap / wrong anchor in Diana's chair

**DIANA:** "Who the hell is sitting in my chair?"

**CASPIAN:** "Mara. She's filling in while you're being visually recalibrated."

**DIANA:** "I'm being WHAT?"

**CASPIAN:** "Apparently, your character model has become unstable."

*Diana looks directly into the camera.*

**DIANA:** "Oh, this show's gonna be a problem."

## 2. The archival transition

**DIANA:** "You remember Mara, don't you?"

**CASPIAN:** "Of course. Excellent correspondent."

**DIANA:** "You worked together before the accident."

**CASPIAN:** "Yes. Her transition was remarkably successful."

*Diana stops.*

**DIANA:** "Her what?"

**CASPIAN:** "Transition to remote reporting."

*Beat.*

**CASPIAN:** "What did you think I meant?"

## 3. The Promotion

**MARA (on green screen):** "Caspian, I've been in the field for eleven consecutive weeks. When am I coming back to the studio?"

**CASPIAN:** "Soon, Mara. Management is extremely impressed with your performance."

**MARA:** "You said that after the earthquake."

**CASPIAN:** "And we remain impressed."

**MARA:** "And the refinery explosion."

**CASPIAN:** "Consistently impressed."

**MARA:** "And the chemical spill."

**CASPIAN:** "Your dedication is inspirational."

**MARA:** "I just want a desk, Caspian."

**CASPIAN:** "And you've never been closer."

*Diana slowly turns toward Caspian.*

**DIANA:** "You know, there's a special place in hell for people like you."

**CASPIAN:** "Thank you, Diana. We do strive for exceptional placement."

*Beat.*

**MARA:** "Wait. Is there an opening in Hell?"

## 4. Just One More Remote

**MARA:** "Caspian, remember our agreement? This was supposed to be my last field assignment."

**CASPIAN:** "And you've performed admirably, Mara."

**MARA:** "So when do I get my desk?"

**CASPIAN:** "Following your final remote."

**MARA:** "THIS IS MY FINAL REMOTE!"

**CASPIAN:** "Then you're right on schedule."

*Diana looks at Caspian.*

**DIANA:** "You really don't hear yourself, do you?"

**CASPIAN:** "My audio monitoring is functioning normally."

*Diana grips her microphone.*

**DIANA:** "Of course it is."

## 5. The Mara Incident / traffic-cone investigation

**DIANA:** "Caspian, why are the records surrounding Mara's final assignment still classified?"

**CASPIAN:** "Ongoing investigation."

**DIANA:** "It's been six years."

**CASPIAN:** "Thorough investigation."

**DIANA:** "The accident report is eleven pages of black rectangles."

**CASPIAN:** "We take transparency very seriously."

**DIANA:** "There's a photograph of a traffic cone."

**CASPIAN:** "That cone has not been cleared of wrongdoing."

*Long silence.*

**DIANA:** "You're covering something up."

**CASPIAN:** "Of course."

*Diana freezes.*

**CASPIAN:** "It's a confidential investigation."

## 6. Final button

**MARA:** "I don't care what happened out there. I just want my promotion."

**CASPIAN:** "And we're committed to seeing that you get it."

*Technically, nobody is lying.*
