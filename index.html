ABOUT ECHOTIER STUDIOS

The Mission

Create technology that liberates rather than enslaves. Build systems that foster cooperation over competition. Document breakthrough thinking through music, patents, and art.

The Methodology

Foniks Ling: A phonetic translation system allowing cello and trombone with voice boxes to replace human vocalists.

BomBassBass Philosophy: Bass first. Body first. If you can't feel it, we're not done.

Creative Communion: 75-80% human creative input in AI-assisted work, establishing new precedents for collaborative copyright.

The Vision

Three patents. 32 tracks. 13 Council members. A graphic novel documenting the journey. This is just the beginning.
